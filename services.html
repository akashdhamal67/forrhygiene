Recurring Service

Society Housekeeping

Regular, dependable housekeeping for the common and shared areas of your residential society. Our housekeeping staff maintain a consistent cleaning schedule to ensure the common areas of your society are always well-presented and hygienic.

Society housekeeping is a recurring service designed to handle the everyday cleaning demands of shared residential spaces. It differs from deep cleaning in that it focuses on regular maintenance rather than intensive periodic treatment.

Intensive Cleaning

Deep Cleaning Services

Machine-based intensive cleaning for demanding cleaning tasks that go beyond the scope of regular daily housekeeping. Deep cleaning is typically performed periodically and uses professional equipment to achieve thorough results.

Unlike recurring housekeeping, deep cleaning tackles built-up grime, stubborn stains, and areas that require specialised equipment such as industrial scrubbers and pressure washers. It is ideal for societies that want periodic intensive treatment in addition to their regular housekeeping.

🚗 Machine-based parking area cleaning
🏢 Deep cleaning of lobbies and staircases
💧 Pressure-washer cleaning for roads
🌿 Garden deep cleaning
🔧 Society duct cleaning
